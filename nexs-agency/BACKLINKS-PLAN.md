# Earning genuine backlinks for napnix.in

Read this honestly: **no one can "create" genuine backlinks.** A backlink is
another site's owner linking to you. The only real levers are (1) listings you
verify as the owner, (2) links you earn through outreach, PR and content, and
(3) credit links from people you've worked with. Everything else sold as
"backlink creation" is spam, and on a domain registered 2026-05-25, during the
September 2026 spam update, spam links are a liability, not an asset.

This file is the earned-link plan: drafted and ready to send. Foundation
citations (GBP, Bing, Clutch, directories) are in CITATIONS.md — do those first.

Ranked by value for a four-month-old software agency.

---

## 1. Client credit links — highest value, you control the ask

A "Built by Napnix" credit in the footer of a site you built is the most
relevant backlink an agency can have: topical, from a real business, earned.
Your public portfolio names real engagements — Taxiologists, the NapCRM
manufacturing and legal clients. Each is a potential link.

**The snippet to give each client** (small, tasteful, in their footer):

```html
<a href="https://napnix.in" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Built by Napnix</a>
```

**The ask (email/WhatsApp to each client):**

> Hi [name] — quick one. We're listing [their project] in our portfolio and
> we'd love a small "Built by Napnix" credit in your site footer linking to
> napnix.in. Takes a minute, helps both of us with search visibility, and I'm
> happy to add it myself if you can give me access. Either way — thank you for
> the work.

If any of these client sites are ones you host/deploy, you can add the credit
yourself. Do NOT add it to sites you don't own or without asking — a credit
link the client didn't agree to is the wrong move.

Realistic yield: 2–5 links, every one relevant and genuine. Worth more than
100 directory listings.

- **Justdial** — justdial.com/Mohali (real local intent)

nobody visits passes no trust and clusters you with spam.

## 3. Digital PR — earned editorial links

The durable way to earn DA. Each needs you (a real person, a real quote) but
the asset and pitch can be prepared.

- **HARO / Featured / Qwoted** — answer journalist queries on software, CRM,
  Indian startups, AI. A quote in a real article = a strong editorial link.
  Set a daily 10-minute habit; respond as the founder.
- **Founder bylines** — a genuine article on building NapCRM, or lessons from
  the Taxiologists fleet build, pitched to YourStory, Analytics India Magazine,
  Dev.to, Hashnode, IndiaCRM blogs. The Taxiologists story is real and
  specific — that specificity is what gets accepted.
- **Local tech press** — Chandigarh/Mohali startup coverage (Tricity startup
  roundups, Punjab IT association). A new local software firm is a story.

I can draft any of these as full pieces when you pick one.

## 4. Linkable assets on napnix.in — build the link magnet

The scalable way to "create" backlinks: publish something genuinely useful that
people cite. This is real work I can build in the repo:

- A free tool in your niche (a CRM-cost calculator, a lead-response-time
  checker, a "is your SPA crawlable" tester — you'd dogfood the last one).
- A small original data study (e.g., response-time benchmarks across service
  businesses) — original data earns links better than anything.
- Open-source a genuinely useful utility under the Napnix GitHub org with
  napnix.in in the README and package homepage. github.com links are mostly
  nofollow, but they drive referral traffic, aid discovery, and read as a real
  engineering footprint.

Say the word and I'll build one of these.

## 5. Partnerships and memberships — genuine, relevant

- Tech you're certified/partnered in (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud partner
  directories) — if you hold the certs, these are authoritative dofollow links.
- NASSCOM / local IT association membership pages.
- Being listed as an agency by tools you implement (CRM/stack vendors often
  have partner pages).

---

## What NOT to do (it will hurt this domain)

- No paid link packages, PBNs, "1000 backlinks for $X", comment/forum link
  spam, or mass directory blasts.
- No reciprocal-link schemes across unrelated sites.
- No link velocity spikes — a new domain gaining hundreds of links in days is
  the exact footprint the spam update targets. Earn them steadily.
